GloorpFlorp - private screen sharing and voice chat for two PCs
================================================================

Full guide: open "GloorpFlorp Guide.html" in this folder.

1. Keep everything in this folder together. GloorpFlorp.exe needs the
   "ffmpeg" and "mpv" folders next to it. Don't run it from inside the zip:
   extract the whole zip first (right-click the zip > Extract All).

2. Install Tailscale on both PCs and sign in: https://tailscale.com/download
   Both PCs must show up in Tailscale. If you use two different Tailscale
   accounts, one of you shares their PC with the other ("Share" in the
   Tailscale admin console). The person who shared their PC clicks Host.

3. Double-click GloorpFlorp.exe.
   - "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   - Windows Firewall asks for permission: tick BOTH "Private" and
     "Public", then click "Allow access".

4. One of you clicks Host and sends the code (like K7QM-2XPA-9TD) to the
   other. The other pastes the code and clicks Join.

5. To show your screen: pick a monitor, then click Start sharing.
   Your friend's screen opens in its own window. Press f or double-click
   it for fullscreen. Close it any time; reopen it with "Open viewer".

6. To send your game's sound: under "Game audio" pick "Everything except
   GloorpFlorp" or "Only one program", then click "Start sending game
   audio". Leave stops your sharing and game audio.

Something wrong? See "Troubleshooting" in the guide. When asking for help,
send your log file: press Windows+R, paste %APPDATA%\GloorpFlorp and press
Enter, then send log.txt from that folder (and log.old.txt, the log from
before GloorpFlorp was last restarted).

You and your friend need the same version of GloorpFlorp (see VERSIONS.txt).
