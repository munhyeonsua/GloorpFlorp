Third-party software in GloorpFlorp
===================================

This folder contains two separate open-source programs, FFmpeg and mpv.
GloorpFlorp starts them as their own processes and talks to them through
pipes; they are unmodified builds made by their packagers. Both are licensed
under the GNU General Public License (GPL). You may copy and share them
under the terms of the GPL. The GPL also gives you the right to get their
source code: the links below lead to the exact source of the builds in this
folder. VERSIONS.txt lists the exact versions.

{{RELEASE_SOURCES}}


FFmpeg  (ffmpeg\ffmpeg.exe)
---------------------------
Used for: capturing your screen and encoding the video with NVENC.
License:  GNU GPL version 3 or later. This is a "gpl" build, made with
          FFmpeg's --enable-gpl and --enable-version3 options, that also
          includes other GPL-compatible libraries (for example x264 and
          x265).
          License text: licenses\FFmpeg-LICENSE.txt (the GNU GPL version 3,
          the same text as licenses\GPL-3.0.txt)
Built by: BtbN's automated FFmpeg builds.
Source:   FFmpeg, at the exact version of this build:
            {{FFMPEG_SOURCE}}
          Build scripts for this build. They pin the exact version of every
          library included and say where its source comes from:

mpv  (mpv\)
-----------
Used for: showing your friend's screen in the viewer window.
License:  mpv's own code is under the GNU GPL version 2 or later. This
          Windows build also contains its own copy of FFmpeg (again made
          with --enable-gpl and --enable-version3) and other GPL libraries,
          so this build as a whole is under the GNU GPL version 3 or later.
          License texts: licenses\GPL-3.0.txt and licenses\GPL-2.0.txt
          (and any licenses\mpv-* files)

GloorpFlorp.exe
---------------
GloorpFlorp's own code is under the MIT license
(licenses\GloorpFlorp-LICENSE.txt). Its source code is linked in
VERSIONS.txt ("Built from"). GloorpFlorp.exe is built from the open-source
Rust libraries ("crates") listed below, each under the license shown in
brackets (mostly MIT and/or Apache-2.0). It also contains
libopus, the Opus audio codec (BSD 3-Clause license, https://opus-codec.org),
built into the opusic-sys crate. Each crate's license text can be found on
its page at https://crates.io.
