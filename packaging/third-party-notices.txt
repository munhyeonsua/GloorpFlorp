Third-party software in GloorpFlorp
===================================

This folder contains two separate open-source programs, FFmpeg and mpv.
GloorpFlorp starts them as their own processes and talks to them through
pipes; they are unmodified builds made by their packagers. Both are licensed
under the GNU General Public License (GPL). You may share them under the
terms of the GPL, which includes passing on where to get their source code
(the links below). The exact builds are listed in VERSIONS.txt.


FFmpeg  (ffmpeg\ffmpeg.exe)
---------------------------
Used for: capturing your screen and encoding the video with NVENC.
License:  GNU GPL version 3 or later. This is a "gpl" build that also
          includes other GPL-compatible libraries (for example x264 and x265).
          License text: licenses\FFmpeg-LICENSE.txt
Built by: BtbN's automated FFmpeg builds.
Source:   FFmpeg:                       https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
          Build scripts and the exact
          versions of all libraries:    https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds


mpv  (mpv\)
-----------
Used for: showing your friend's screen in the viewer window.
License:  GNU GPL version 2 or later. This Windows build also contains
          FFmpeg and other libraries.
          License text: licenses\GPL-2.0.txt (and any licenses\mpv-* files)
Built by: shinchiro's automated mpv builds for Windows.
Source:   mpv:                          https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv
          Build scripts and the exact
          versions of all libraries:    https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake

built into the opusic-sys crate. Each crate's license text can be found on
